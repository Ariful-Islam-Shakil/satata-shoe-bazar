# Requirement Notes: Satata Shoe Bazar
**Date:** 2026-05-06
**Source:** Requirement analysis conversation with client
**Status:** Ready for developer review

---

## 1. Project Overview
Satata Shoe Bazar is a dedicated e-commerce website for a single shoe store based in Bangladesh. It allows the owner to sell shoes directly to customers, providing a clean shopping experience with easy discovery and order management.

## 2. Users & Roles
The platform will have two main roles:
*   **Customer:** Can browse shoes, search and filter products, add items to a wishlist, place orders using Cash on Delivery, track order status, and leave reviews/ratings.
*   **Admin (Owner):** A single administrator (the store owner) who manages the entire platform, including inventory, orders, shoe categories, and responding to customer reviews.

## 3. Seller & Product Management
This is a single-seller platform, so there is no seller onboarding process. The owner will list physical shoes only. Products will be organized by brand and categories like "New Arrivals" and "Best Sellers." Out-of-stock items will remain visible on the site but will be clearly marked with an "Out of Stock" badge.

## 4. Buyer Experience
Buyers can find products through a search bar and filters for size, color, and brand. The homepage will feature highlights like "New Arrivals" and "Best Sellers." On the product page, buyers will see multiple photos, prices, size charts, material descriptions, and customer reviews. Buyers can also save shoes to a wishlist for later.

## 5. Payments & Orders
The platform will exclusively support Cash on Delivery (COD) for the initial launch. Shipping is handled with a flat delivery fee within Bangladesh. The order lifecycle will include stages such as "Order Received," "Packaging," "Out for Delivery," and "Completed." Customers can track these stages through their account.

## 6. Reviews & Ratings
Customers can leave star ratings and text reviews for shoes they have purchased. Reviews will appear on the site immediately without prior approval. The store owner has the ability to reply to these reviews publicly.

## 7. Admin & Platform Management
The owner will have a dashboard to monitor total sales, manage orders that need to be processed/delivered, and update the shoe catalog (adding/editing products and categories).

## 8. Business Rules
*   **Return Policy:** There is a "no return" policy for all purchases.
*   **Delivery:** Flat-rate shipping applies to all orders within Bangladesh.
*   **Stock:** Products remain visible even when out of stock.
*   **Promotions:** No discount vouchers or promo codes are planned for the initial launch.

## 9. Launch Priorities
The primary goal is to launch a functional e-commerce site with the core features (catalog, filtering, wishlist, COD checkout, and basic admin dashboard). The expected initial user base is approximately 50-60 customers.

## 10. Open Questions
*   **Cancellations:** While there is a "no return" policy, it is unclear if customers can cancel an order before it has been shipped.
*   **Specific Regions:** While the delivery is "flat-rate," it should be confirmed if there are different rates for Dhaka vs. outside Dhaka, as is common in Bangladesh.
